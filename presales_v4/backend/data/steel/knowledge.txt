# Requirement for RFID-Based Solution for HR Coil Yard Facility

Requirement for RFID-Based Solution for HR Coil Yard Facility

HR Yard-1:

Concrete flooring with ~540 saddles for double-stacking HR coils.

Operations are carried out using a 45 MT Goliath crane.

HR Yard-2:

Raw surface without flooring and saddles.

Currently used for single-stacking HR coils due to uneven surface.

Current Pain Points:

After weighment, stickers are manually pasted on coils (risk of errors or wrong placement).

During issue of HR coils to user departments, identification is done manually by a person who marks coils with chalk.

This process is time-consuming and requires significant manpower, impacting daily operational efficiency.

Requirement:

We need a smart RFID-based solution to:

Eliminate manual sticker pasting.

Automate identification and marking of coils for lifting.

Save time and resources while improving accuracy and efficiency.

Expected RFID Features:

Durable RFID Tags: Capable of withstanding outdoor conditions, dust, and temperature variations.

Long-Range Readability: Tags should be readable from a distance suitable for crane operations.

Integration Capability: System should integrate with existing weighbridge and yard management software.

Real-Time Tracking: Ability to locate and identify coils instantly within the yard.

Error Prevention: Ensure correct coil identification during lifting and dispatch.

Cost-Effectiveness: Affordable solution without compromising reliability and performance.
